About HIVE

Most buyers groups are built by a few builders pooling their spend to get better pricing, then opening the deal to others.

HIVE flips that approach. We work directly with suppliers to understand their business, reduce their risk, and help grow their market share — in return, we secure top-tier pricing and fairer terms for our members. We're not builders ourselves — we specialise in procurement and contracts. And we know from experience that a well-balanced, high-trust ecosystem outperforms a strong-arm deal every day of the week.

We're funded by our suppliers — not our members. Every supplier pays the same flat rate to be part of the Hive. That means we're not chasing commission, upsell, or promoting one supplier over another.

We only win when both sides win: members save and stick around, suppliers grow — and everyone plays fair.

We choose suppliers based on value, alignment, and loyalty — not margins. If a supplier puts a great offer on the table, we'll make sure you know about it. But whether you take it or not is completely up to you.

Membership

Yep. No sign-up fees. No monthly charges. No contracts. You can join for free, and you're free to leave any time. We keep things simple because we back what we offer — no traps, no tricks.

Just three things: Buy fair. Pay fair. Give fair, accurate feedback — and tell your mates.

All HIVE members agree to a Code of Conduct — it keeps the network running smoothly and protects your access to top-tier deals. You don't need to jump through hoops, but we do expect you to back the system that backs you.
