Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy is currently under legal review. The finalised version will be published here once our legal advice is received. In the meantime, this page outlines our current privacy practices in plain language.

Who We Are

HIVE Trade Network Pty Ltd (ABN 21 683 196 018) operates the HIVE Trade Network — a collective buying network for independent Australian trade businesses. We are based on the Central Coast, NSW.

For privacy enquiries, contact us at admin@hivetradenetwork.com.

What Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you express interest in joining HIVE or partnering as a supplier. This includes:

  • Your name and business name
  • Contact details (email, phone)
  • ABN and business location
  • Trade type and purchasing patterns
  • Transaction data relating to purchases made through HIVE supplier accounts

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Match you with relevant supply partners
  • Administer your HIVE membership
  • Calculate and issue supplier fee invoices
  • Communicate updates, savings reports, and operational notices
  • Meet our obligations under our supplier agreements (MSPA)

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant details with supply partners for the purpose of establishing and maintaining your trade account. We do not share your information with any third party for marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Australian and international cloud services. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at admin@hivetradenetwork.com. We will respond within 30 days.
