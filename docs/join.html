How it works

How joining HIVE works

No shortcuts. No fluff. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you sign up.

Before you start — what you'll need
Accounting software — Xero, QuickBooks, or MYOB
Job management — Buildxact or equivalent

Not set up yet? That's fine — we'll help you get sorted as part of the process.

1
Deep dive questionnaire

No shortcuts. We go through your business in detail — your trade, your team, your current suppliers, what you're spending and where, how your books are sitting, what your goals are. This is how we find every move to put money back on the table. The more you give us, the better your plan.

2
Your HIVE plan gets built

Beyond Bookkeeping takes your questionnaire data and gets to work — running a full Financial Health Assessment across your balance sheet, cash flow, margins, super obligations, and BAS status, benchmarked against your trade sector. Your systems get scored across six dimensions. You receive a Financial Performance Report and Systems Health Score. Parts 1 and 2 are free to every HIVE member.

3
Connect your accounting software

Whether you're on Xero, QuickBooks, or MYOB, we get it connected and clean. Purchases, invoices, reconciliation — all flowing automatically. Automatic super payments, BAS tracking, ATO compliance indicators. No manual entry. No end-of-month scramble.

4
Get Buildxact integrated and loaded

We load your HIVE supplier price lists directly into Buildxact. From there, Buildxact handles the full workflow: running your takeoffs from plans, auto-generating your bill of materials, converting estimates to customer-ready quotes, and firing off purchase orders ready for your suppliers.

5
Full access to the HIVE supplier network

As a HIVE member, you're granted access to every supply partner in the network. Not a curated shortlist. Not a limited selection based on your trade. Every vetted HIVE supplier, every category, every deal — your membership is your key in.

6
From here, the game changes.

Every part of the system is firing. HIVE pricing at the counter, books automated, super sorted. Buildxact handling your quoting, bill of materials, and purchase orders. Beyond Bookkeeping has your back-office built the right way — automated, integrated, systemised. You know your margins. You know exactly what you're saving. You're quoting sharper, buying smarter, and running leaner than the bloke next to you who's still doing it the old way.

You've been leaving money on the table long enough. The system's set. The pricing's live. Now let's go get it back.

Five minutes to fill in. A lifetime of better buying.
