The standard

We're not here to pit suppliers against each other.
We're here to raise the bar.

HIVE exists to give independent trade businesses the kind of supplier relationships they've always deserved — fair, consistent, and genuinely valued. We're looking for supply partners who share that vision.

That means more market share — and a trusted pipeline of SME trade customers who are aligned, loyal, and ready to buy.

Exclusive by design

HIVE selects up to two suppliers per material class per location — not to restrict access, but to focus it. If you become a preferred supplier, you'll earn more than just orders. You'll earn long-term trust — and market share that's protected.

What makes a great HIVE supplier
  • You fight for the right to serve small businesses that have been overlooked for too long
  • You offer a strong overall value proposition — pricing, product range and quality, service, and fairness
  • You want to help tradies hit their business goals, grow sustainably, and reap the rewards of a successful business
  • You see loyalty as a two-way street — and you're willing to earn it

When our members recognise the value in your offer, they don't need to shop around or play the field — and we won't encourage them to. Sound like you?
