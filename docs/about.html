Why we exist

We're here to back the trade — not compete with it.

Independent trade businesses keep the country running. But when it comes to buying power, they've always been stuck playing second fiddle to the big end of town. HIVE flips that script.

We're a trade-first buying group that gives small and medium businesses real leverage — better deals, less admin, and more respect. The goal is simple: help good operators run lean, reduce waste, and use quality materials that keep rework low and margins high — so there's more left over at the end of the week for what really matters.

Our Mission

To empower small to medium trade businesses by leveraging collective buying power to secure unbeatable terms and pricing, streamline procurement processes, and enhance operational efficiency. HIVE is dedicated to fostering equitable opportunities for suppliers and members by promoting loyalty, integrity, collaboration, and mutual growth — ensuring a fair and sustainable industry ecosystem.

Our Vision

To become the leading national platform that bridges the gap between suppliers and small to medium trade businesses — driving loyalty, long-term value, and sustainable growth for every player in the network.

How we work

No product pushing.
Just bottom-line growth.

Unlike suppliers who profit by moving more product, HIVE has one goal — to help you spend less and earn more. We're not here to sell you things you don't need. We exist to make sure when you do buy, you're getting the best value, with the least friction, and zero runaround.

We do the background work — negotiating supplier deals, simplifying logistics, and holding the line on quality — so you can stay focused on the job, confident you're buying smart every time.

What makes HIVE different

Not a marketplace.
Not a wholesaler.
Your corner.

Most buyers groups are built to serve whoever funds them. We only make money when our members save and our suppliers grow — so we're permanently pointed in the same direction as you.

We select suppliers based on value, alignment, and loyalty. We don't promote one over another based on who pays more — because they all pay the same flat rate. That's the whole model.
